        Rubrics · Formula Engine · Syllabus Transparency

        How you are graded: Your Final Grade = Class Standing (CS) x 60% + Periodical Test (PT) x 40%. CS reflects your daily consistency; PT reflects your competency execution. Both are built from five regimental components tracked in this dashboard. A negative merit balance then directly subtracts from this total — see Merit Override Penalty below.

        Official Assessment Rubrics

        Component Weight Excellent (90–98) Satisfactory (75-89) Needs Improvement (<75)
        CLASS STANDING (CS) — 60% of Final Grade
        Daily Logbook RecordingMARPOL Compliance & Transparency
        		 30% of CS
        = 18% of FG
        		 All daily entries submitted on time. Perfectly legible, using precise SMCP terminology with complete MARPOL Garbage Record Book documentation. Zero missed logs for the term. Most entries submitted on time with only minor omissions or informal phrasing. At most 1-2 missed entries made up within the same week. 3 or more unresolved missing entries per term. Entries are illegible, contain factual errors, or lack MARPOL waste disposal records entirely.
        Events & FormationsMusters, Assemblies & Mandatory Events
        		 30% of CS
        = 18% of FG
        		 100% attendance at all musters, assemblies, and mandatory regimental events. Always in proper uniform. Demonstrates initiative during formations — reports promptly and falls in first. At most 1-2 excused absences per term with proper documentation. Generally prompt and in correct uniform. Minor lapses corrected immediately when noted. Three or more absences from mandatory events without valid excuse. Habitual tardiness at formation. Repeated uniform violations during assemblies.
        Merits / Patriotism & DisciplineFlag Raising · Flag Lowering · Uniform
        		 40% of CS
        = 24% of FG
        		 Merit balance near maximum (450). Perfect attendance and decorum at every Flag Raising and Flag Lowering ceremony (+10 merits each). Zero demerits for the entire term. No uniform violations. Positive merit balance maintained. Attends Flag ceremonies regularly with few exceptions. Minor demerits (-10 to -30) for correctable uniform violations such as unshined shoes (-10) or bucklebelt (-10). Net negative merit balance. Frequent absences during Flag Raising/Lowering. High demerit accumulation (-100+) from violations: improper haircut (-50), absent (-50), or improper uniform (-30). Any negative balance also triggers the Merit Override Penalty below — it is no longer limited to lowering this row's own 24% share.
        Merit Override Penalty — applies on top of everything above A negative merit balance does not only lower the Merits row's own 24% share of your grade — it directly subtracts from your entire Final Grade, after Class Standing, Periodical Test, and any bonus have already been added. The penalty is 3 percentage points for every -100 merits, with no floor — a severely negative balance can fail a term outright even with excellent academics, logbooks, and mockboat performance. Example: -1,000 merits subtracts 30 points from your Final Grade; -2,000 merits subtracts 60 points. There is no maximum to how far this can reduce a grade.
        PERIODICAL TEST (PT) — 40% of Final Grade
        Mockboat Duties & LeadershipChecklist Execution · Watch Officer Role · Drills
        		 70% of PT
        = 28% of FG
        		 As Officer: Exemplary bridge command — issues and repeats helm orders without hesitation. Leads team through emergency drills (Fire/Lifesaving) flawlessly. Checklists: 90-100% of duty tasks completed per Day profile. Perfect surface preparation, knot execution, and 5S compliance. Serves as officer competently with minor coaching needed. Completes 70-89% of duty checklist tasks. Drill responses are timely but not perfectly coordinated. Basic knots and mooring executed correctly. Fails to assume officer role effectively. Less than 70% of duty checklist completed. Repeated hesitation during drills. Poor tool handling. Fails to tie essential knots within time limits.
        Academic GWA / Star CadetCross-Course Average · Theoretical Mastery
        		 30% of PT
        = 12% of FG
        		 Star Cadet (GWA >= 90): Top academic achiever across all major maritime courses. Earns automatic +2 bonus on Final Grade. Masterful theoretical knowledge of COLREG, STCW, SOLAS, and MARPOL. Maintains a standard passing GWA (75-89) across all major courses. Understands core maritime theoretical concepts adequately. Passes all term examinations. GWA falls below 75. Fails one or more major maritime theory examinations (Navigation, Stability, MARPOL). Requires academic intervention.

        Competency Appraisal 1
        Corequisite: PIC 267 | Prerequisite: None
        Course Outcomes
        CO1

        Execute a continuous 4-hour bridge/deck duty rotation demonstrating mastery in watchkeeping, visual lookout, and formal relief procedures (STCW A-II/1).

        CO2

        Perform shipboard maintenance and seamanship tasks — surface preparation (de-rusting), painting, knottings, and mooring operations (STCW A-II/5).

        CO3

        Communicate using SMCP and INTERCO through physical flag hoisting and identification.

        CO4

        Demonstrate professional discipline and safety compliance by correctly utilizing PPE and maintaining accurate Deck Logbook and Garbage Record Book entries.

        STCW Knowledge (KUPs)
        A-II/1 KUP1: Thorough knowledge of COLREG 72; Bridge Resource Management.
        A-II/1 KUP2: Knowledge of MARPOL; Anti-pollution procedures and equipment.
        A-II/1 KUP3: Ability to use INTERCO; Hoisting single-letter signals.
        A-II/5 KUP4: Painting, lubrication, cleaning materials; Surface preparation.
        A-II/5 KUP5: Mooring equipment and procedures; Ropes, wires, knots/splices.
        A-II/5 KUP6: Blind pilotage and lookout duties; SMCP.
        A-VI/1 KUP7: Emergency procedures; Safe working practices on board ship.
        Class Policies
        1.

        Watchkeeping Attendance: Missing a 4-hour duty without valid excuse = "Failure to Report" and failing grade for that module.

        2.

        PPE Mandatory: Full PPE (Helmet, Coverall, Safety Shoes, Gloves) required. No PPE = No Entry to the mockboat work area.

        3.

        Chain of Command: Maritime chain of command must be observed. Disrespect handled under institutional disciplinary code.

        4.

        Tool Integrity: Loss or damage of tools due to negligence must be reported immediately to the instructor.

        5.

        AI Use Policy: AI tools may assist with research only. Cannot produce final drafts or be used during practical duty simulations.

        6.

        INC / IP Marks: IP for ongoing research. INC given for failure to take final exam or non-submission of major requirements per OVPA memo.

        Course Outcomes
        CO1

        CO2

        Demonstrate technical proficiency in deck maintenance — systematic chipping, cleaning, and painting of ship structures. (Table A-II/5)

        STCW Knowledge (KUPs)
        A-VI/1 KUP1: Life-saving appliances; Donning of lifejackets and immersion suits.
        A-VI/1 KUP2: Fire drills and emergency signals; Portable fire extinguishers.
        A-II/5 KUP3: Surface preparation techniques; Chipping, de-rusting, marine coatings.
        A-II/1 KUP4: Safe rigging and boarding arrangements; IMO/IMPA pilot ladder standards.
        A-II/5 KUP5: Routine equipment checking; Preventative maintenance schedules (PMS).
        A-VI/1 KUP6: Abandon ship drills; Survival craft launching and recovery procedures.
        A-II/5 KUP7: Deck cleanliness principles; Tool inventory and 5S methodology.
        ICPE Summative Assessment — PIC 267
        Watchkeeping & Navigation (CO1 — 30%): Bridge Resource Management; Helm orders; Lookout reporting with relative bearings; Formal Relief of Watch with vessel status, weather, and current orders reporting.
        Maintenance & Seamanship (CO2 — 30%): Systematic de-rusting (chipping) and marine coating application; Speed and accuracy in tying 10 essential maritime knots; Correct mooring line handling and securing to bitts/cleats.
        Communication & Signaling (CO3 — 20%): Accuracy and speed in identifying, hoisting, and lowering INTERCO flags; SMCP utilization for deck-to-bridge and simulated VHF reporting; Absolute clarity and brevity in technical communications.
        Discipline, Safety & Documentation (CO4 — 20%): Strict PPE adherence (Helmet, Coverall, Safety Shoes, Gloves); Technical accuracy of Deck Logbook entries; Correct MARPOL Garbage Record Book documentation; 5S implementation and tool integrity throughout the rotation.
        Class Policies

        All six class policies from PIC 136 apply identically to PIC 267: mandatory watchkeeping attendance, full PPE compliance, chain of command observance, tool integrity accountability, restricted AI use during practical simulations, and INC/IP grade mark procedures per OVPA memo.
