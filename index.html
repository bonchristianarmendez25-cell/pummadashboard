        Rubrics · Formula Engine · Syllabus Transparency

        How you are graded: The official university grade sheet expresses your Final Grade as Class Standing (CS) x 60% + Periodical Test (PT) x 40% — CS for daily consistency, PT for competency execution. The live "Specific Grade %" on this dashboard is computed from the same five regimental components, but combined with the different weights shown in Step 6 of the walkthrough below (Academic 10% / Leadership 25% / Patriotism 25% / Soft Skills 25% / Technical 15%) and converted through the Rating → Percent table — it is not a simple re-expression of the 60/40 split, so don't expect a hand-entered CS/PT pair to reproduce the dashboard number exactly. A negative merit balance then directly subtracts from this total — see Merit Override Penalty below.
        What "Officer" means on this dashboard: The Officer flag (toggled per cadet, shown as OFFICER badge in the table) refers specifically to membership in the Corps of Midshipmen — the regimental command body documented in the PUMMA Regimental Rules (the "Triple R" handbook linked from the top banner). A cadet flagged as Officer is serving in a leadership post such as RCC, RDCC, RA, or an N-line billet (N1–N4), and is held to the Corps' "Covenant of Honor" standard of conduct. This flag directly raises Leadership CS (see Mockboat Duties & Leadership row below) from 3 to 5, because Corps officers are graded on their command performance, not just task completion.

        Why the Excel Numbers Look Different from the Dashboard

        When you click "Download Panpacific Excel" or "Load All Cadets → Download", the CS and PT columns written to the file are not the same raw scores you see on this dashboard. This is intentional. Here is why:

        Dashboard display (raw scores)
        CS = 89.31  (out of 100)
        PT = 91.28  (out of 100)
        If copied raw → 89 + 91 = 180 → #### ERROR
        Excel export (pre-weighted portions)
        CS = 89.31 × 0.60 = 53.59
        PT = 91.28 × 0.40 = 36.51
        T = 53.59 + 36.51 = 90.10 ✓ (no error)
        Why the university sheet works this way

        The university's Excel grade sheet uses a formula that literally adds = CS + PT. Its column header labels "Class Standing (60%)" and "Periodical Test (40%)" mean it expects each column to already contain the weighted portion — not the full raw score. By pre-multiplying CS × 0.60 and PT × 0.40 before writing to the file, the dashboard ensures the university sheet's formula always produces a valid T (max ≈ 98.0) and the FG cell never shows #### or an error, no matter how high the cadet's scores are.

        CS column in Excel
        raw CS × 0.60
        Max value ≈ 58.8
        PT column in Excel
        raw PT × 0.40
        Max value ≈ 39.2
        T column (auto-sum)
        CS + PT
        Max value ≈ 98 ✓

        The FG (Final Grade) column is not affected — it is always the correctly rounded term grade and is the same value shown on the dashboard.

        Complete Step-by-Step Grading Engine — Every Calculation, In Order

        This is the exact sequence the dashboard's grading engine runs for every cadet, every time data changes. Provided for academic audit purposes — every number on a cadet's grade card traces back to one of the numbered steps below.

        Step 1 — Determine the Merit Cap (live, never fixed)
        Merit Cap = 150 + (No. of Papers × 10)

        The Merit Cap is recalculated automatically from No. of Papers (set in General Config) every single time it changes — it is never a hardcoded number. With the current setting of 30 papers, the cap is 450. This cap is the ceiling against which the Merits component (Step 4) and Excess Merit Bonus (Step 7) are measured.

        Step 2 — Academic CS (1–5 scale)
        IF Star Cadet flag is ON → Academic CS = 5.0 (fixed)
        ELSE IF Mean GWA ≥ 90 → Academic CS = 5
        ELSE IF Mean GWA ≥ 85 → Academic CS = 4
        ELSE → Academic CS = 3

        Mean GWA is the average of every non-zero semester GWA entered (1st, 2nd, 3rd Sem). A cadet flagged Star Cadet (GWA ≥ 90 academic excellence award) automatically locks Academic CS at the maximum, regardless of any later GWA edits, AND separately earns a flat +2 percentage point bonus on the Final Grade (see Step 8).

        Step 3 — Leadership CS (1–5 scale) — Corps of Midshipmen Officer Status
        Leadership CS = Officer flag ON ? 5 : 3

        The Officer flag means the cadet currently holds a post in the Corps of Midshipmen (RCC, RDCC, RA, or an N-line billet N1–N4 per the PUMMA Regimental Rules / Triple R handbook) — not their rotating mockboat duty rank. Holding a Corps post is a binary switch: it does not scale gradually, it simply raises Leadership CS from the baseline 3 to the maximum 5.

        Step 4 — Patriotism / Merits CS (1–5 scale, continuous)
        Peer Ceiling = "Dynamic" mode → highest total merits in the section/class
                      "Fixed" mode → the Merit Cap itself (Step 1)
        Merit Ratio = Total Merits ÷ Peer Ceiling
        IF Total Merits > 0 → Patriotism CS = MIN(5, 1 + MAX(0, Merit Ratio) × 4)
        IF Total Merits ≤ 0 → Patriotism CS = 1 ÷ (1 + |Total Merits| ÷ 200)

        This produces a smooth, continuous score on the 1–5 display scale — a cadet with -25 merits and a cadet with -2,100 merits do not get the same "1" rating; the second formula spreads negative balances out so worse balances visibly read worse, even though both ultimately also trigger the separate whole-grade penalty in Step 9.

        Step 5 — Technical CS & Soft Skills CS (Mockboat Checklists + Logbook)
        Technical Pass Rate = (Technical tasks completed ÷ Technical tasks assigned) × 100
        Technical CS = ≥90%→5 · ≥80%→4 · ≥70%→3 · ≥60%→2 · else→1
        Soft Skills Pass Rate = (Soft tasks completed ÷ Soft tasks assigned) × 100 → same 5/4/3/2/1 banding
        Logbook % = Valid logged weekdays ÷ Expected weekdays in graded months (Sat/Sun excluded)
        Log Score = ≥80%→5 · ≥60%→4 · ≥40%→3 · ≥20%→2 · else→1
        Soft CS (final) = ROUND((Soft Skills Pass Rate score + Log Score) ÷ 2)

        Technical and Soft Skills tasks come from whichever Checklist Profile(s) are assigned to the cadet's section under Map Duties. The displayed Soft CS blends checklist soft-skill completion with logbook diligence in equal parts.

        Step 6 — Base Rating (weighted average of all five components, 1–5 scale)
        Base Rating = (Academic × 0.10) + (Leadership × 0.25) + (Patriotism × 0.25) + (Soft × 0.25) + (Technical × 0.15)

        Note: this is the formula that actually computes the live "Specific Grade %" shown on the Class Records table. It is not a re-expression of the CS/PT 60/40 split shown in the rubrics tab and summary cards above — the two use different effective weights per component (e.g. Technical sits at 15% here vs. an effective ~28% under "Mockboat = 70% of PT, PT = 40% of FG" in that framing). The CS/PT 60/40 view is the official university export/term-grade format; this five-component Base Rating is the dashboard's own live calculation. Treat them as two related but distinct views of performance, not two paths to an identical number.

        Step 7 — Excess Merit Auto-Bonus
        Excess Merits = MAX(0, Total Merits − Merit Cap)
        Auto-Bonus % = MIN(5, (Excess Merits ÷ 10) × Bonus-Per-10 Rate)

        Merits earned above the cap aren't wasted — they convert into a percentage bonus, at the configurable Excess Merit Bonus Rate (default 0.5% per 10 excess merits), capped at +5 percentage-points-equivalent.

        Step 8 — Combine All Bonuses & Convert Rating → Percent Grade
        Total Bonus % = Manual Instructor Bonus + Auto-Bonus (Step 7) + (Star Cadet ? 2.0 : 0)
        Bonus-as-Rating = Total Bonus % × (0.49 ÷ 2.00)
        Final Rating = MIN(5.00, Base Rating + Bonus-as-Rating)
        Percent Grade = banded conversion of Final Rating (see Rating→Percent table below)

        Bonuses are converted into an equivalent nudge on the 1–5 rating and applied before the rating is converted to a percent — not added directly to the percent afterward. This keeps two cadets with genuinely different ratings from being artificially flattened to the identical 98% ceiling; the conversion factor (0.49 rating points per 2.00 percent points) mirrors the slope of the top rating band itself.

        Step 9 — Apply the Merit Override Penalty (final step, whole-grade)
        IF Total Merits < 0:
          Merit Penalty = (|Total Merits| ÷ 100) × 3
          Percent Grade = Percent Grade − Merit Penalty
        Final Grade = CLAMP(Percent Grade, 0, 98)

        This is the very last operation, applied after Steps 1–8 are fully resolved. There is no floor on the penalty itself — only the final grade is clamped to a 0–98% range — so a deeply negative merit balance can override an otherwise excellent academic and leadership record.

        Fully Worked Numeric Example

        Sample cadet: GWA 92 (Star Cadet), Officer = YES, Total Merits = +520, 30 Papers configured (Merit Cap = 450), Bonus Rate = 0.5%/10 excess, Technical Pass 95%, Soft Pass 92%, Logbook 88%, Manual Bonus = 0%.

        1. Merit Cap = 150+(30×10) = 450
        2. Academic CS = Star Cadet → 5.0
        3. Leadership CS = Officer=YES → 5
        4. Merit Ratio = 520÷450 = 1.156 → Patriotism CS = MIN(5, 1+1.156×4) = 5.0
        5a. Technical CS: 95%→5
        5b. Soft Pass 92%→5, Log 88%→5 → Soft CS = ROUND((5+5)/2) = 5
        6. Base Rating = (5×.10)+(5×.25)+(5×.25)+(5×.25)+(5×.15) = 5.00
        7. Excess Merits = 520−450 = 70 → Auto-Bonus = MIN(5,(70÷10)×0.5) = 3.5%
        8a. Total Bonus % = 0 + 3.5 + 2.0(Star) = 5.5%
        8b. Bonus-as-Rating = 5.5×(0.49÷2.00) = 1.3475 → Final Rating = MIN(5,5.00+1.3475) = 5.00 (capped)
        8c. Percent Grade (5.00 rating, top band) = 98.00%
        9. Merit Penalty: Total Merits is positive → no penalty applied
        FINAL GRADE = 98.00% (Outstanding)

        Admin Export Reference — Panpacific Excel File Explained

        This section explains every value the "Download Panpacific Excel (.xlsx)" button writes into the university's official grade sheet. Intended for faculty, academic officers, and admins who need to verify, submit, or audit the downloaded file.

        Why CS and PT in the Excel are smaller than on the dashboard

        The dashboard's CS and PT values are raw percentage scores (0–100). The university's grade sheet template, however, uses a formula that simply adds = CS_cell + PT_cell, expecting each column to hold the already-weighted portion. The system therefore pre-multiplies before writing:

        CS written to Excel = raw dashboard CS × 0.60   → max ≈ 58.8
        PT written to Excel = raw dashboard PT × 0.40   → max ≈ 39.2
        T (university formula adds them) = max ≈ 98  ✓ No overflow, no #### error

        This matches the intent of the university sheet's own column labels — "Class Standing (60%)" and "Periodical Test (40%)" — without requiring any change to the university template.

        What to do if a number looks wrong in the Excel
        • CS and PT columns look low — This is correct. They are the weighted portions (×0.60 and ×0.40). Verify the FG column instead, which is the grade on record.

        Officer Status & the Corps of Midshipmen

        — per the PUMMA Regimental Rules (Triple R)

        The dashboard's Officer toggle (per cadet, in the cadet profile editor) is a direct digital record of Corps of Midshipmen membership — the student-officer command body that runs PUMMA's regimental system. Per the Regimental Rules, the Corps is "a professional association of student-officers who have been vetted and selected to lead their peers through the rigors of maritime discipline," operating under a stricter "Covenant of Honor" than the general cadet body.

        Typical Corps Posts (Officer = YES)
        • RCC — Regimental Commandant of Cadets (highest officer)
        • RDCC — Regimental Deputy Commandant (Chief Officer)
        • RA — Regimental Adjutant (Chief Operating Officer)
        • N1.1–N1.3, N2, N3, N4 — N-line staff officers (Operations, Intelligence/Security, Welfare, Logistics)
        • Battalion / Platoon command staff, Class Marcher, Guidon bearer
        Effect on Grading
        • Leadership CS jumps from baseline 3 → 5 (Step 3 of the engine)
        • Counted toward the Officer Count stat shown in class/section summaries
        • Shown as an OFFICER badge across the Cadet Table, Masterlist, and printed appraisal cards
        • Independent of Mockboat duty rank (Master/Captain down to Ordinary Seaman) — a cadet can be a rotating Mockboat "Captain" for the day without holding a standing Corps post, and vice versa

        "Leadership is a weight, not a rank" — every midshipman officer is personally accountable for the performance and morale of the cadets under their charge, which is why the rubric rewards Officer status as the maximum Leadership CS score rather than a partial credit.

        Excel: =IFERROR(ROUND(T,0),"")
        Cap & Override
        FG is capped at 98 (the system's absolute ceiling) and floored at 0. A negative merit balance subtracts directly from FG after rounding (Merit Override Penalty — see Rubrics tab), so T and FG can legitimately differ by more than just rounding once a penalty applies.

        CS is itself the dashboard's full Base Rating converted to a percentage (Academic 10% + Leadership 25% + Patriotism 25% + Soft Skills 25% + Technical 15%, see Rubrics tab). T is the WEIGHTED BLEND of CS (60%) and PT (40%), never a plain sum — weighting first guarantees T can never exceed 98, since CS and PT are each already capped at 98 individually.

        How the Excel Export Writes CS & PT — Admin Reference

        The university's Excel template uses a plain = CS + PT sum. Its column headers "Class Standing (60%)" and "Periodical Test (40%)" mean it expects each cell to already contain the pre-weighted portion, not the raw 0–100 score. If the raw score (e.g. CS 89, PT 91) were copied directly, the sum would be 180 — which Excel displays as #### or an overflow error.

        To prevent this, the "Download Panpacific Excel" function automatically multiplies before writing:

        CS written to Excel
        raw CS × 0.60
        e.g. 89.31 → 53.59
        PT written to Excel
        raw PT × 0.40
        e.g. 91.28 → 36.51
        T (university formula)
        = CS + PT
        53.59 + 36.51 = 90.10 ✓

        The FG (Final Grade) column is unaffected — it is always the correctly rounded term grade, identical to what the dashboard displays. The CS and PT columns in the Excel will look smaller than the dashboard values; this is correct and expected. Do not adjust them manually.
